WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

September 10, 2016, on a dark night, my sister and i decided to test her newly purchased camera. We rushed to our rooftop in palestine, set up the camera on a tripod,and started getiing some results. although they were quite average, we were amazed of how those picture turned out. From that night, my passion for photography grew bigger and bigger. and the more time i spend the more i realize how joy photography brings me.
